USAGE:
	[MAZE] | ./viewer
	./viewer < [FILE]
	./viewer -m (to launch the maze maker)

	MAZE: data of a solved maze.
	FILE: a file containing data of a solved maze.

DESCRIPTION:
	This program acts as a visualizer for the A-Maze-d project.

	The viewer reads, on the standard input, the data of a maze,
	parsed and solved by the A-Maze-d program.
	It will display a representation of the given maze, with all
	of its rooms and tunnels, and simulate the robots' movements
	from the start to the end of the maze.

	The maze maker lets you build your own maze, from start to
	finish. Rooms and tunnels can be placed wherever you wish,
	with no limit to how many of them the program can handle.
	Once done, you can export your maze, which will be written
	inside of a file, located in your current directory.

USER INTERACTIONS:
	VIEWER:
		Play / Pause...................	'SPACEBAR'
		Rewind to start................	'BACKSPACE'
		Next move......................	'N' or '+'
		Previous move..................	'B' or '-'
	MAKER:
		Place room.....................	'LMB'
		Destroy room...................	'LCTRL'
		Create tunnel..................	'LALT' (hold and drag to next room)
		Place end room.................	'E'
		Add a robot....................	'N' or '+'
		Remove a robot.................	'B' or '-'
		Save maze......................	'S'
	GENERAL:
		Toggle logs....................	'TAB'
		Move camera....................	Arrow keys or 'RMB' + 'Mouse move'
		Zoom camera in.................	'C' or 'Up scroll'
		Zoom camera out................	'X' or 'Down scroll'
		Speed up camera................	'LSHIFT'

		Quit program...................	'ESCAPE'
