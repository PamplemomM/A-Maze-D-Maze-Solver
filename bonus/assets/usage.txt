
USAGE:
	[MAZE] | ./viewer
	cat [FILE] | ./viewer
	./viewer < [FILE]

	MAZE: data of a solved maze.
	FILE: a file containing data of a solved maze.

A-MAZE-D VIEWER:
	This program acts as a visualizer for the A-Maze-d project.
	It reads, on the standard input, the data of a maze, parsed
	and solved by the A-Maze-d program.
	It will display a representation of the given maze, with all
	of its rooms and tunnels, and simulate the robots' movements
	from the start to the end of the maze.

USER INTERACTIONS:
	Play / Pause...................	'SPACEBAR'
	Rewind to start................	'BACKSPACE'
	Next move......................	'+'
	Previous move..................	'-'

	Focus on a robot...............	'LMB'
	Focus on next robot............	'Right arrow'
	Focus on previous robot........	'Left arrow'
	Disable focus..................	'Down arrow'

	Toggle freecam.................	'F' or any of the following
	Move camera....................	'LMB' + 'Mouse move'
	Zoom camera in.................	'Up scroll'
	Zoom camera out................	'Down scroll'

	Quit program...................	'ESCAPE'
